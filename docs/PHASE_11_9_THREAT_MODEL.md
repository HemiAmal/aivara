## 1. Threat Modeling Methodology

Phase 11.9 integrates heterogeneous evidence, findings, confidence metrics, and risk scores across AIVARA's verification subsystems. Adversaries and failure modes may attempt to subvert the aggregation pipeline, inflate or suppress risk, manipulate policy thresholds, forge provenance chains, or exploit double-counting vulnerabilities.

We evaluate threats across the entire lifecycle:
$$\text{Evidence Ingestion} \longrightarrow \text{Validation \& Deduplication} \longrightarrow \text{Ancestry Clustering} \longrightarrow \text{Finding Synthesis} \longrightarrow \text{Risk Aggregation} \longrightarrow \text{Decision Dispatch}$$

## 2. Comprehensive 30-Scenario Threat Matrix

| Scenario ID | Threat Category | Threat Description | Attack Vector / Trigger | Impact | Mitigation Strategy | Residual Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TM-11.9-001** | Evidence Injection | Adversary injects fabricated evidence records into the integration pipeline. | Direct submission of forged JSON payloads without cryptographic binding. | False risk inflation or spurious quarantine. | Strict RFC 8785 signature verification and ancestor hash validation against database. | Attacker with database write access could inject records; mitigated by OS/DB ACLs. |
| **TM-11.9-002** | Evidence Deletion / Stripping | Adversary strips critical shift evidence to force an `ACCEPT` disposition. | Man-in-the-middle payload truncation during batch aggregation. | False sense of security; deployment of shifted assets. | Complete evidence set hashing (`evidence_set_hash`) and audit ledger cross-referencing. | None within air-gapped system boundaries. |
| **TM-11.9-003** | Evidence Replay | Adversary replays historical clean evidence to mask current distribution shift. | Resubmitting prior evidence hashes with updated timestamps. | Bypasses drift detection on degraded/poisoned datasets. | Cryptographic binding to current `comparison_boundary_hash` and `audit_run_id`. | Historical replay with identical boundary is mathematically identical. |
| **TM-11.9-004** | Sybil Evidence Duplication | Adversary duplicates identical evidence records under multiple IDs. | Generating multiple UUIDs for identical underlying statistical tests. | Artificially inflated risk score due to duplicate counting. | Deterministic deduplication by `evidence_hash` = SHA-256(RFC8785(data_json)). | Duplication across differing slight perturbations handled by ancestry clustering. |
| **TM-11.9-005** | Evidence Source Spoofing | Adversary asserts false source identity or engine ID on evidence payloads. | Modifying `engine_id` or `source_id` in untrusted input records. | Unjustified attribution of anomalies to innocent contributors. | Strict separation of claimed source metadata from verified provenance; engine ID validation. | Claimed metadata remains unverified unless signed. |
| **TM-11.9-006** | Provenance Chain Forgery | Adversary creates forged parent-child evidence linkages. | Fabricating `previous_event_hash` or parent evidence IDs. | Corruption of audit trails and explainability graphs. | Cryptographic hash chaining; parent existence and digest verification. | None; hash collisions computationally infeasible. |
| **TM-11.9-007** | Finding Severity Manipulation | Adversary modifies severity from `CRITICAL` to `INFO`. | Modifying JSON payload fields before risk aggregation. | Suppression of high-impact security warnings. | Strict immutable schema validation (`FindingModel.frozen=True`) and finding digest binding. | Internal memory corruption requires local privilege escalation. |
| **TM-11.9-008** | Confidence Inflation | Adversary alters detection confidence from $0.20$ to $0.99$. | Modifying numerical confidence field in transit. | Unwarranted quarantine of benign natural variations. | Recomputing confidence strictly from statistical p-values ($1 - p_{\text{adj}}$) or proof layer rule. | P-value computation authority resides in Phase 11.3 engine. |
| **TM-11.9-009** | Multi-Modal Double Counting | Correlated detectors on the same image inflate composite risk by $5\times$. | Phase 11.4, 11.5, 11.6, 11.7, 11.8 observing same underlying asset change. | False alarm fatigue and unwarranted asset rejection. | Ancestry-aware evidence clustering with inter-modality damping factor $\lambda_{\text{corr}} = 0.10$. | Slight non-linear residual correlation in unmodeled higher moments. |
| **TM-11.9-010** | Risk Score Suppression | Adversary tampers with risk policy weights to force a zero risk score. | Modifying risk configuration file on disk. | Undetected drift; critical security blind spot. | Cryptographic binding of `risk_policy_hash` in `IntegratedAssuranceProfile`. | Unauthorized config edits produce immediate policy hash mismatches. |
| **TM-11.9-011** | Decision Threshold Tampering | Adversary increases quarantine threshold $\tau_{\text{quarantine}}$ to $1.0$. | Tampering with decision policy configuration. | All quarantine actions silently downgraded to `ACCEPT`. | Cryptographic binding of `decision_policy_hash` in audit profile. | Requires policy change authorization and audit event logging. |
| **TM-11.9-012** | Conflicting Evidence Suppression | Engine suppresses negative or contradictory evidence to present a biased report. | Filtering out $H_0$ acceptance results ($p > 0.05$). | Skewed assurance perception and false confidence. | Mandatory reconciliation: all executed tests must be represented in `evidence_records`. | None; accounting is exhaustive. |
| **TM-11.9-013** | Stale Evidence Abuse | Ingesting old, non-applicable baseline evidence to evaluate current dataset. | Mismatched dataset version or expired baseline cache. | Inaccurate risk assessment based on obsolete distribution. | Baseline version and timestamp binding; strict `dataset_version_id` matching. | Requires user to maintain up-to-date baseline registrations. |
| **TM-11.9-014** | Cross-Project Leakage | Evidence from Tenant A influences risk assessment or decision for Tenant B. | Mixed tenant IDs in batch evaluation request. | Cross-tenant data contamination and privacy violation. | Strict `ProjectMismatchError` assertion across boundary, contract, and evidence. | Multi-tenant isolation verified by automated tests. |
| **TM-11.9-015** | Cross-Dataset Contamination | Aggregating evidence from Reference Dataset into Target Dataset evaluation. | Accidental cross-binding of population identities. | Incorrect drift calculation and corrupted baseline profiles. | Explicit `PopulationIdentity` binding and cryptographic boundary hashing. | None. |
| **TM-11.9-016** | Cross-Model Contamination | Integrating model weight anomalies from Model A into Model B risk profile. | Incorrect `model_id` / `model_fingerprint` in evidence linkage. | Spurious model quarantine. | Verification of `model_master_fingerprint` across all model evidence. | None. |
| **TM-11.9-017** | Contributor PII Leakage in Findings | Ingesting raw contributor emails/names into global finding descriptions. | Formatting finding title with unpseudonymized user string. | Privacy breach; GDPR/HIPAA violation. | Strict project-scoped pseudonymization (`derive_project_scoped_pseudonym`) in all finding details. | Raw PII rejected at canonicalization stage. |
| **TM-11.9-018** | Cryptographic Digest Collision Attack | Constructing distinct evidence records with identical SHA-256 hashes. | Second pre-image attack on SHA-256. | Forged evidence accepted as authentic. | Use of standard RFC 8785 Canonical JSON Serialization (JCS) + SHA-256 (256-bit security). | Cryptographically negligible risk ($2^{-128}$). |
| **TM-11.9-019** | Resource Exhaustion (DoS) | Submitting $100,000$ evidence items to exhaust memory during aggregation. | Unbounded evidence array in API request. | Process crash / out-of-memory denial of service. | Hard resource ceiling: $E_{\max} = 1000$ evidence items, $F_{\max} = 200$ findings per run. | Requests exceeding ceilings immediately rejected. |
| **TM-11.9-020** | Malformed JSON Payload Injection | Injecting deeply nested or NaN/Inf floats in `data_json`. | Malformed numeric payloads designed to break JSON serializers. | Parser crash or undefined numeric comparisons. | Strict Pydantic parsing with finite float validation (`math.isnan`, `math.isinf` sanitized). | None. |
| **TM-11.9-021** | Unsafe Deserialization Attack | Injecting serialized Python objects in metadata dictionaries. | Submitting pickle / eval exploits in metadata strings. | Remote code execution. | 100% standard `json` parsing; zero `pickle`, `eval`, or `exec` in entire codebase. | Verified by automated AST security scanners. |
| **TM-11.9-022** | Offline Boundary Violation | External telemetry or remote policy fetching during risk calculation. | Inadvertent import of `requests`, `httpx`, or socket libraries. | Information leakage in air-gapped secure environments. | 100% offline self-contained execution; zero network libraries imported. | Verified by automated import scanner. |
| **TM-11.9-023** | Historical Decision Rewriting | Altering a past decision in the database after policy update. | Retroactive mutation of stored `RiskAssessment` records. | Audit fraud and loss of regulatory compliance. | Immutability of past records; policy updates generate distinct new profile instances. | Read-only ledger enforcement. |
| **TM-11.9-024** | False-Safe Fallacy ("Absence of Evidence") | Interpreting 0 findings on an unanalyzed dataset as "Certified 100% Safe". | Missing test coverage or skipped analysis modules. | Unwarranted deployment of unverified models. | Explicit `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` status; safety is never certified from null tests. | System displays unanalyzed dimensions explicitly. |
| **TM-11.9-025** | False-Malicious Attribution | Labeling a statistical distribution shift as "Confirmed Dataset Poisoning". | Non-causal drift interpreted as deliberate attack. | Unjustified legal/contractual liability for contributors. | Core non-attribution invariant: descriptive language only (`"distribution shift observed"`). | Enforced across all finding templates. |
| **TM-11.9-026** | Causal Overclaiming | Asserting that Source X caused downstream model accuracy drop. | Inferring causality from observational correlation without RCT. | Misleading root cause remediation. | Explicit caveat attached to conditional shift findings; no causal DAG claims. | Clear limitation documented in assurance profiles. |
| **TM-11.9-027** | Proof Dilution by Statistical Noise | Using high statistical p-values ($p = 0.90$) to override a cryptographic hash break. | Averaging proof layer scores with detection layer scores. | Critical security breach masked by benign distribution. | Proof violations trigger immediate mandatory `REJECT` disposition regardless of detection risk. | Proof layer is strictly non-compensable. |
| **TM-11.9-028** | Uncalibrated Probability Exposure | Presenting risk score $0.75$ to user as "75% probability of backdoor". | Misrepresenting composite exposure index as posterior probability. | Misleading risk governance and compliance reporting. | UI/API contracts define risk score strictly as `Operational Exposure Index in [0.0, 1.0]`. | Explicit documentation in schema descriptions. |
| **TM-11.9-029** | Conflicting Modality Deadlock | Feature drift says Shift, Representation drift says No Shift. | Inconsistent sensitivity across abstraction layers. | Pipeline hang or unhandled exception. | Explicit `DISCREPANT_MODALITY_EVIDENCE` aggregation state with review recommendation. | Review queue surfaces both modal results to operator. |
| **TM-11.9-030** | Sub-Threshold Sybil Smuggling | Splitting shifted data across 50 small sources ($N=20$) to bypass $N_{\min}=30$. | Sybil fragmentation attack. | Stealthy distribution poisoning. | Phase 11.8 reconciliation tracks sub-threshold volume; emits `EXCESSIVE_SOURCE_FRAGMENTATION`. | Emits advisory when sub-threshold volume $>20\%$. |

## 3. Threat Model Summary & Security Posture

The Phase 11.9 architecture comprehensively mitigates all 30 threat scenarios through:
1. **Cryptographic Binding**: Complete content-addressed hashing (RFC 8785 + SHA-256).
2. **Layer Separation**: Absolute isolation of non-compensable Proof Layer violations from Detection Layer metrics.
3. **Ancestry Clustering**: Heavy damping of multi-modal correlated evidence sharing raw asset lineage ($\lambda_{\text{corr}} = 0.10$).
4. **Safe Defaults**: Strict fail-closed error handling (`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` and `REVIEW` instead of `ACCEPT`).
5. **Strict Semantic Invariants**: Absolute prohibition of unsupported causal attribution or maliciousness claims.
