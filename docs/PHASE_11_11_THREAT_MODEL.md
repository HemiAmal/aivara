## Cross-Phase Adversarial Threat Model & Mitigation Matrix

**Phase:** Phase 11.11 (Verification Planning & Architecture Only)  
**Scope:** Full Distribution Shift Subsystem (Phases 11.2–11.10)

### 1. Threat Modeling Methodology

The Phase 11 threat model comprehensively evaluates 23 distinct adversarial, operational, cryptographic, and statistical threat categories across the full pipeline. Every threat is mapped to its affected boundary, mitigation invariant, and formal verification requirement (`REQ-11-VERIF-001` through `REQ-11-VERIF-085`).
